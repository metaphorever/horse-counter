## Decisions made
- **Neck option 2** (pivot down 25u, neck +20u). *Clover, picked from rendered options.* The pivot is the contract, but nothing is baked yet, so moving it now is cheap.
- **Squash the bumper dome now.** *Clover.*
- **Graze solved from the art, not a fixed pair of angles.** *Claude.* **Complexity justification:** Clover will keep redrawing the neck and head. Fixed angles would drift off the grass after every edit; the solver keeps Graze honest the way hoof IK keeps stances honest. ~20 lines.
- **Grazing head angle −30° world.** *Claude.* A tuning knob, not art.
- **New file `Edit2.svg` instead of rewriting `Edit.svg`.** *Claude.* Clover's uncommitted tail work lives in `Edit.svg` in the main checkout; a new file means no merge conflict when she pulls.
