### How the template was made
- **The pieces are cut out of the production art, not redrawn.** So the neutral pose *is* today's horse, which gives the harness a built-in correctness check: in the chip rows, the "current pose" row matches the 2.4 row.
- **Joint heights come from the production legs' width profiles:**
  - fore: elbow 214, knee 260, fetlock 316;
  - hind: stifle 214, hock 262, fetlock 320.

  Cross-checked against Clover's walk drawings (fore knee ~255–270, hind hock ~262). Each pivot sits mid-leg at its cut, with a cap of half the width + 1u.
- **Neck root pivot deep in the chest at (187, 145)**, for the longest lever at graze. A full-width cap there (r=57) made the chest a ball. So the cap is r=46, and the band just past the cut that it can't reach belongs to the static front bumper. The neck then turns with no corners swinging out.
- **Ground 344.5, not the spec's 342.** The far legs now reuse the near shapes, and the ground has to match the straight fore leg: making it 0.5u shorter costs ~11° of knee bend. Nothing moves on the live site.
- **Tails** are bent about the dock by moving the path's control points, so they stay smooth curves. The rump part inside the barrel is left alone.

### Verified in the browser
- **Neutral pose:** fore joints ~0°; the hind is within 5° (it reaches 0.8u down to meet the fore's ground line).
- **6 stances:** every hoof within 1.2u (0.3px) of the ground.
- **Walk (defaults):** 0 joint-limit violations over 16 frames. Stance hooves float 0u. Body bob 0–11.5u, ~3px at chip scale.
- **Every joint at both limits:** the silhouette stays in one piece. Neck raise is capped at +20°, because past that the chest front notches.
- **Illustrator-style round trip:** `_x2D_` escapes, a `head1`-style duplicate suffix, class-based styles, a `style="display:none"` layer, and a transformed wrapper group. Result: all 14 pieces found, pivots/ground/toe shifted by exactly the wrapper's (10, 5), the eye kept its white, the neck took the coat.
- **Bugs caught and fixed during the build:**
  - startup ran before the embedded data had been read;
  - the knee side was forced from the fore's bend direction (replaced with limit-aware choice nearest neutral);
  - swing aimed the toe with a ground-relative tuck, so the forearm over-reached to ~100° (replaced with a fetlock arc plus a cannon-relative tuck);
  - the swing fetlock wasn't clamped;
  - raised heads went off the top of the stage.
