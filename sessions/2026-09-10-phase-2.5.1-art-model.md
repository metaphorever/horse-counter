## What shipped

No code. **A committed spec amendment** — Rule 2 gate for the changed art contract.

**1. Reviewed Clover's hand-drawn walk cycle** (`prototypes/horse-chip-art-walk.svg`,
8 frames × 4 colour-coded legs). Measured all 32 leg drawings via a browser harness
(bbox, lowest point, hoof x/y, belly-line span) rather than by eye.

**Headline finding: it is a trot, not a walk.** Normalised hoof position is
effectively identical frame-for-frame between fore-near/hind-far and
fore-far/hind-near — both diagonal pairs land and lift in unison, 4 frames apart.
A walk is 4-beat LH→LF→RH→RF.

| frame | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fore-near | .62 | .84 | 1.0 | .70 | .31 | .07 | 0 | .22 |
| hind-far | .57 | .81 | 1.0 | .76 | .37 | .07 | 0 | .14 |
| fore-far | .33 | .15 | 0 | .23 | .60 | .76 | 1.0 | .69 |
| hind-near | .40 | .17 | 0 | .14 | .57 | .69 | 1.0 | .76 |

Would have been fixable **without redrawing** — playing the fore set 2 frames early
turns support from `2,2,0,2,1,2,0,2` feet down into a textbook `3,2,3,2,3,2,3,2`
with bipedal phases alternating lateral/diagonal. (Shifting +2 instead gives a
diagonal-sequence walk — a camel, not a horse.) Superseded by the art-model change
below, but recorded because the corrected gait is the **target** for the posing work.

**Three further defects, one root cause** (frames drawn in isolation → ground
reference and body anchors drift):

| Defect | Measured |
|---|---|
| Stance hooves not on one ground line | 8 of 32 drawings within 3u of y=339; stance ends float 4–13u |
| Uneven stance spacing (skating) | 17.8u–63.8u per frame, should be ~38u; fore stride 156–161u vs hind 145–147u |
| Hind legs skim | peak clearance 20u hind vs 50u fore |

**Other measurements banked:** near/far sets agree to a mean of 3.2u vertical /
4.3u horizontal (so they *are* one motion — outlier: hind-near frame 6 sits ~17u
forward). Production ground line is **y≈342**, walk cycle drifted to 339 (~1px —
re-check `--hz-y` after any swap). Stride 304u = 1.3× wither height, anatomically
correct, which is *why* it overflows a short chip. `.hz` is **26px on every
surface sitewide** — the Fancy full-width work widened the container, not the horse.
Chip-scale render vs production baseline: leg tangle is denser but chips already
overlap; an increase in an existing condition, not a new problem.

**2. Amended `spec/phase-2.5.1-static-pose-variation.md`** — art model changed from

**3. Amended `spec/phase-2.5-horse-animation-overview.md`** — asset shape, build

**4. Banked the gait reference kit** in `prototypes/`: the walk SVG, an animated

## Decisions made

- **Posable segments for authoring, baked flat frames for runtime** —
  *Clover proposed, Claude approved (with amendment), Clover confirmed.* Clover
  raised posable off the back of the trot finding; Claude added the build-time
  baking amendment. Decisive reason is **correctness, not perf**: "No-JS core, JS
  as a treat" is a spec commitment and 2.5.1's variety is server-emitted, so
  runtime JS posing would break it. Baked symbols keep the runtime **identical to
  the already-approved plan**. Clover restated the reasoning independently
  (including the JS point) — genuine understanding, not an ok.
  **Not a one-way door:** segment data survives, so promoting a part to runtime
  posing later (carrot toy, head tracking) is additive.
- **~10 pieces, chains start at elbow and stifle** — *Claude proposed, Clover
  confirmed.* Bumpers are **structural**: hiding shoulder and hip means the two
  hardest joints never have to merge. Static haunch is a simplification with a
  named fallback (promote to a 4th hind segment) — **Clover checks at first prototype.**
- **Tail stays hand-drawn, 4 positions** — *Claude pushed back on Clover's
  "head and tail posable too", Clover revised.* Swish is timing, not pose
  vocabulary; a segmented chain is where a flowing shape most reads as a puppet.
- **Neck + head split into 2 segments** — *Claude proposed, Clover confirmed.*
  Justified specifically by Graze: a rigid head+neck points the muzzle down at
  grazing depth instead of levelling at the ground.
- **16 frames for the walk; standing pool is separate and purpose-built** —
  *Claude steelmanned, changed Clover's position.* Clover proposed 32–64 inbetweens.
  Displayed frames per cycle = fps × stride duration, so at the low frame rate we
  want, 32+ bakes frames nobody sees. But the instinct was right for a different
  reason: **static variety has no such cap**, and a standing horse is not a walk
  frame held still — a purpose-built standing pool is what 2.5.1 actually ships and
  reads better than paused cycle frames.
- **Clover's 32 drawings are reference, not deliverable** — *Clover confirmed
  explicitly.* No corrected-SVG pass, no interim 2.5.1 on current art. They become
  the gait target (posing aims at Clover's own hoof positions, corrected) and the
  source of segment proportions.
- **Claude ships the parts template first, with placeholders already posed** —
  *Claude proposed, Clover confirmed.* Clover styles over proven geometry and sees
  a wrong pivot immediately, rather than the sprint landing on bad geometry.

## Uncertainty flags

- **Uncertain:** whether segmented limbs hold the 2.4 chunky hand-cut look in
  motion. **Claude cannot self-assess this** — measuring a generated SVG is
  reliable, judging "reads as a puppet" is not. Partly de-risked by the single-scale
  finding (26px everywhere ⇒ joint rounding is sub-pixel), but that answers the
  geometry question, not the motion question. *Resolved by:* Clover's eyes on the
  first posed prototype. **Open.**
- **Uncertain:** whether numerically-derived joint angles read as lifeless next to
  hand-drawn frames. *Resolved by:* pose tables are a tunable angle list Clover can
  scrub (same idiom as `--hz-y`). **Open, mitigated.**
- **Uncertain:** `filter: drop-shadow` per chip re-rasterises on any change inside
  it — the likeliest cause of 2.5.2 stutter at herd scale. Cost is identical for
  frame-swap and runtime posing, so it did **not** decide this session's call.
  *Resolved by:* live test before committing to gait art; frame rate is the cheap
  lever (15fps ≈ ¼ the cost of 60fps). **Open, deferred to 2.5.2.**
